## Allowed Without a Commercial License

You may use Colcoor without a commercial license for:

- Personal use.
- Educational use.
- Academic research.
- Internal, non-production evaluation by organizations.
- Copying, modifying, forking, and redistributing the source under the terms of
  the Business Source License 1.1.
- Contributing changes back to this repository.

## Requires a Commercial License

Organizations need a commercial license before using Colcoor for:

- Production use.
- Offering Colcoor, or substantial Colcoor functionality, as a hosted or
  managed service.
- Embedding Colcoor into a commercial product.
- OEM distribution.
- Selling services or products based on Colcoor.

In general, if an organization is using Colcoor to run a real business
workflow, serve customers, support paid products, or generate revenue, contact
us first for commercial licensing.
